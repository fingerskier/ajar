**"How God Moves a Business Through 4 Levels of Wealth"** by Dan Romer (channel linked to Goshenites / Order of Kings material).

The core idea is that wealth isn’t primarily about the dollar amount in the bank but about four distinct *ranges* (domains/scopes) measured by the ratio of resources to the assignment/task you’re carrying. You don’t leave a range mainly by earning more; you leave it by changing behavior and operating under different laws.

### The four ranges (with biblical anchors)
1. **Not enough / lack** — Hebrew *ḥāsēr* (“to lack / be without”). Psalm 23’s “I shall not want” (*lōʾ ʾeḥsār*) is framed as an exit declaration from this range.
2. **Perfectly enough / sufficiency** — Hebrew *day* (“enough”). Tied to the name El Shaddai (often read as the God who said “enough” and set creation’s limits). Sufficiency is a blessing, but the category itself has a built-in ceiling.
3. **More than enough / surplus** — Illustrated by Exodus 36:7 (*dayām wəhōtēr* — “sufficient and more than sufficient / surplus”). *Hōter* means what remains after the need is met.
4. **Much more than enough / hyper-abundance** — Paul’s language in Ephesians 3:20 (*hyper* + *ek* + *perissos* = above and beyond, overflowing from abundance). This is treated as a different category of existence, not just “more of the third range.”

### How you move between them
- **Out of lack** → Law of *order* (Proverbs 21:20 contrast: the wise has treasure; the foolish consumes/squanders it). Discipline, accounting, and structure so money stays long enough to compound.
- **Out of mere sufficiency** → Law of *seed* (2 Corinthians 9:6–10). Sowing generously so harvest (surplus) can appear. You can’t consume your way into surplus.
- **Out of surplus into the fourth range** → Law of *sovereignty* / systems (Genesis 1:28 dominion mandate + Deuteronomy 8:18: God gives the *power* / ability — *kōaḥ* — to produce wealth). Build businesses and systems that generate value without you being the bottleneck.

Practical mapping he gives: first→second = margin; second→third = reinvestment; third→fourth = scalable systems. You’re always only one law/door away from the next range.
