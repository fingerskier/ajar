# Different Types of System Arrangements

## Simple

The chain from cause -> effect has few links


## Complex

The chain from cause -> effect has many links


## Complicated

There are many causes for an effect, each chain [possibly] having many links


## Chaotic

There are many causes for many effects


## Strategies
* Tame chaotic systems by looking at a subset of effects and/or causes
* Weld multiple links in the chains
* Weld chains together
